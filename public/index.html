How to Play 😈

  1. Everyone joins the room on their own phone (3+ players is best).
  2. Each round one player is the Darer — they pick one of two dare cards for a random Target.
  3. The Target can Accept it, Dare Back at double stakes (once per game), or Chicken Out.
  4. Chickening out costs you a point, hands one to the Darer, and lands you a punishment card.
  5. Accept and the clock starts. Do the dare before the timer runs out!
  6. Then everyone else votes: Nailed It for full points, Weak Sauce for half.
  7. 🔥 Hotter dares are worth more — up to 8 points for an Unhinged card.
  8. 🛡️ You get 2 free vetoes per game. No penalty, no explanation needed. Use them.
  9. 🌶️ The host can switch on adult decks in the lobby — everyone should be 18+ and on board.
